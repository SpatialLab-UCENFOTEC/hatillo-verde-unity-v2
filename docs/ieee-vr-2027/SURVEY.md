# Hatillo Verde survey — numbers for the poster

Source: `Hatillo Verde Interactivo.csv` · 17 Jul–14 Aug 2026 · 5-point Likert after the browser 3D experience.

- **N = 29 users** (2 of 31 excluded: said they did not participate)
- 8 residents · 16 visitors · 17/29 had never heard of the site

This is **not** a pre–post experiment and **not** a usability test. “Before” items were asked after the experience (retrospective). No control, no age/gender. Post items sit on the ceiling. Do not claim the 3D interface caused the ratings.

## Headline means (after use)

| Measure | M | SD | 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Awareness to restore | 4.90 | 0.31 | 4.76–5.00 |
| Imagined restored future | 4.79 | 0.41 | 4.62–4.93 |
| Hope | 4.83 | 0.38 | 4.69–4.97 |
| Cronbach’s α (13 post items) | .87 | | internal consistency, not a validated scale |

Median is 5 on every post item.

The only contrast that is not a ceiling: residents already knew the place (prior disconnection Mdn 2.5, n=8) vs visitors (Mdn 5.0, n=16), Mann–Whitney p = .011. After the experience, post ratings do not differ by group.
